Q: i don't see the map of romania with counties why?

A: Bug in the choropleth spec: harta_judete() built the Altair chart with .properties(width="container"). Vega-Lite's geographic projection can't compute its fit when the width is "container" and the measured width is 0 at render time - which is exactly the case inside a narrow st.columns cell and/or a tab that isn't the active one (Streamlit renders all tab bodies up front). Result: the map area stays blank while the table and bar chart render fine.

Fix (both dashboards): give the map concrete dimensions - .properties(width=440, height=340) - and pass the data as a proper {"type":"FeatureCollection",...} with format=alt.DataFormat(property="features", type="json") instead of a bare feature list. Q: I want to add a field showing the amount of taxes paid by companies in every year. That can be calculated as brut profit - net profit, is that true? If OK, calculate it. Then create a new analytics cell showing the top 20 tax payers each year.

A: Partly true, with caveats - explained and implemented. In the Romanian P&L (contul de profit si pierdere), the line between "rezultatul brut" (pre-tax result) and "rezultatul net" (after-tax result) is exactly the tax charge, so:
  impozit_pe_profit = rezultat_brut - rezultat_net
  where rezultat_brut = profitul_brut - pierdere_bruta and rezultat_net = profitul_net - pierdere_neta (in each pair one term is 0).
This captures ONLY the profit/income-tax line (impozit pe profit / pe venitul microintreprinderilor + impozit amanat). It does NOT include VAT, excise, payroll contributions, dividend tax or local taxes - so it understates total taxes paid. It can be slightly negative (deferred-tax income, adjustments). NOTE: the source's "profitul_brut/net" columns are the pre-/post-tax RESULT, not gross margin - so the identity holds; it would NOT hold if "brut" meant gross profit.

Q: Calculate the top "impozit cheaters" - companies with very big revenue (cifra de afaceri) but very low (still positive) net profit. How can we do that?

A: Explained the method and added a new analytics cell. There is no way to *prove* evasion from this data - it's a screening flag. The pattern targeted: a firm reporting a barely-positive net result year after year on large revenue (classic taxable-profit "flattening" via transfer pricing, intra-group management fees / royalties / interest, over-invoicing from affiliates).

Method (on impozit_pe_an_l2.parquet, 2020-2025):
- marja_neta = rezultat_net / cifra_de_afaceri_neta.
- Keep only firms with cifra_de_afaceri_neta >= PRAG_CIFRA_AFACERI (default 50M lei) AND rezultat_net > 0 (losses are a separate story).
- Sector benchmark: marja_mediana_sector = median net margin of the firm's 2-digit CAEN division, per year (compare a firm to its own sector, not the whole economy - 1% margin where peers run 8% is more suspect than 1% in fuel distribution where everyone is ~1%).
- profit_sub_norma = max(0, marja_mediana_sector - marja_neta) * cifra_de_afaceri_neta = the extra profit the firm would have reported at its sector's median margin.
- impozit_potential_lipsa = COTA_IMPOZIT (0.16) * profit_sub_norma - a rough estimate.
- marja_sub_prag = marja_neta < PRAG_MARJA_NETA (default 1%).

Considered but dropped: TARA_FIRMA_MAMA (foreign-parent flag) is only ~0.6% populated in bl_bs_sl_l2 - useless. CAEN division *names* are not in N_CAEN.csv (all rows are leaf classes), and the SECTIUNEA lettering in that file is non-standard, so the cell shows the 2-digit division code + the firm's own 4-digit activity name instead.

New cell in analytics-1-firme-rom.ipynb (markdown id 50a94f4f + code id 2a2e7a00, after the "Impozitul pe profit - top 20 platitori" cell). Parameters PRAG_CIFRA_AFACERI / PRAG_MARJA_NETA / MIN_ANI / COTA_IMPOZIT exposed at the top. Outputs: (1) scatter log10(revenue) vs net margin for the latest year, flagged points in red; (2) per-year top-20 flagged firms by profit_sub_norma -> dict cheaters_per_an; (3) aggregate 2020-2025 DataFrame impozit_cheaters (>= MIN_ANI years above the revenue threshold), sorted by cumulative impozit_potential_lipsa, with ani_marja_sub_prag / marja_neta_mediana / marja_neta_std; (4) cronici = firms with margin < prag in >= 3 years and median margin < prag.

Heavy caveats written into the markdown: NOT proof of evasion. Thin (<1-2%) net margins are normal in distribution/wholesale (fuel, pharma, FMCG, grain), food retail, energy supply, commodity trading, price-regulated activities - and that's most of the list. The cumulative-lei score mechanically favours the largest-revenue firms even at near-normal margins (Dacia, OMV Petrom Marketing show up), so it must be read next to marja_neta_mediana and ani_marja_sub_prag, not alone; the `cronici` table is the better-targeted view. rezultat_net is the accounting result, not the fiscal base; micro-enterprises are taxed on turnover; transfer pricing can be fully legal.

Verified the logic on the data (runs ~1s): ~4,900 firms in the aggregate, 255 "cronic". Top cronic names: Metro Cash & Carry, Dr.Max, Interbrands Orbico, Farmexim, Renault Commercial Roumanie, Viterra, Socar Petroleum - all persistent sub-1% net margin at multi-billion-lei revenue. Not committed (previous turn's work was committed by the user as 3609416 "Updates").
